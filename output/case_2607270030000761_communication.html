안녕하세요.

마이크로소프트 애저 기술지원에 연락주셔서 감사합니다. 저는 본 케이스를 지원할 마이크로소프트 기술지원부 양나은 엔지니어입니다.

[요청내용]

Currently, when an Azure alert is triggered, the email is being sent normally, but the SMS is not being received properly.

When checking in the Azure portal, it appears that the message was sent successfully, but it is ultimately not arriving on the phone.

This issue has been occurring for almost 2 months now, and in addition to the action above, it is happening across all action groups in other resource groups as well.

 

[확인결과]

내부적으로 확인결과, action group (ActionGroup-icms-pg057601)이 지난 7/13부터 현재까지의 notification 발송 이력은 모두 complete 이며, 말씀주신 7/25에는 notification이 발송된 이력 자체가 없는 것으로 확인됩니다.

 

혹시 현재도 문제가 발생하는지 여부와 action group에 등록된 번호 7개 모두 수신이 되지 않는 것인지 확인을 부탁 드립니다.
